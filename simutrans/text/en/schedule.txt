<title>Schedule Help</title>

<h1><strong>Schedule</strong></h1>

<p>
The <strong>Schedule</strong> window sets the schedule for a <a href="convoiinfo.txt">consist</a> or a <a href="linemanagement.txt">line</a>.
It lists <a href="station.txt">stops</a>, where consists pick up and drop off goods and passengers, and waypoints, which can be used to determine the route that consists use, with their respective map co-ordinates.
</p>
<p>
The <strong>Schedule</strong> window for a particular consist can be accessed by pressing the "schedule" button in the <a href="convoiinfo.txt">Consist Information</a> window or  the <a href="depot.txt">Depot</a> window.
To open the <strong>Schedule</strong> window for a line, select "New line" or "Update line" in the <a href="linemanagement.txt">Line Management</a> or <a href="depot.txt">Depot</a> windows.
</p>
<p>
When the <strong>Schedule</strong> window is opened from the Consist Information window, the assigned Line (if there is one) is shown at the top of the window.
</p>
<p>
<em>Serves</em> <a href="linemanagement.txt"><em>line:</em></a> This shows the line currently served by the current consist. To change the line served by this consist, click on the left/right-arrow buttons to cycle through the lines, or click on the name box to open a drop-down-list of the lines, then click one to select it.
Click on the name box and type new name to change the name of the line.
</p>
<p>
<em>Minimum load:</em> This is the minimum quantity of passengers or goods that must be loaded at the selected stop before the consist will leave the stop for its next destination.
The percentage figure refers to the total capacity of the consist and not an indiviual type of item carried.<br>
To use this feature, click on a stop in the list to select it, then use arrow buttons to cycle through the percentages, or type in a precise percentage in the text box.<br>
</p>
<em>Max. wait for load:</em> This is the maximum amount of time that a consist will wait at a stop for the load level specified in "Minimum load". If this is set to "off", the consist will wait as long as it takes to get the loading level stipulated. The remaining settings are proportions of game months: at 1/1, the consist will wait up to one game month before departing the stop whether it has reached the specified loading level or not. At 1/512, the consist will wait 1/512th of a month before moving off even if it does not have the specified level of load.<br>
<em>Tip:</em> Use this feature on lines carrying passengers, mail or other time-sensitive loads to make sure that the journey times are not too long if the "minimum load" feature is set.
</p>
</p>
<em>Spacing cnv/month</em>: This option only works if the "Minimum load" option is set. If this option is set in a schedule with a minimum load, the consist will wait until either the minimum load specified or the stipulated departure time is reached. The "spacing" value represents the number of departures from each stop in the schedule stop per game month, and a departure time for each vehicle calling at the stop is automatically calculated based on an even distribution of these times.<br>
<em>Note:</em> This feature is only available for the schedules of lines, not individual consists. The "spacing" setting will not be visible when editing an individual consist's schedule.<br>
<em>Tip:</em> Use this feature to cause consists to spaced evenly throughout a line, reducing waiting times.
</p>
<p>
<em>Conditional skip:</em> If this is set, the stop is skipped when there is nothing to do there: a depot visit is skipped unless maintenance is needed, and a station stop is skipped when the consist is empty. This option has no effect at waypoints or at a station stop where the consist reverses (such as a mirrored schedule end).
</p>
<p>
<em>Speed limit:</em> If enabled, the consist may not exceed the given speed in km/h on its way to this stop.
</p>
<p>
<em>Pick up only / set down only / discharge payload:</em> These restrict loading at the selected stop: load but do not unload, unload but do not load, or fully unload everything on board.
</p>
<p>
<em>Force range stop:</em> This marks the stop as a range stop for replenishment. (Automatic replenishment visits are not yet implemented.)
</p>
<p>
<em>Lay over:</em> If this is set, the consist is fully unloaded and parked in layover at this stop, and no crew salaries are charged for the layover period. This requires a station building that permits layover, and is only available where one exists.
</p>
<p>
<em>Ignore choose on arrival:</em> If this is set, choose signals are ignored while the consist is heading to this destination.
</p>
<p>
<em>Wait for trigger (Before / After wait):</em> If either is set, the consist waits at this stop until the selected condition number (0-15) has been broadcast by another consist: either before carrying out the scheduled waiting and loading time, or after it.
</p>
<p>
<em>Send trigger on arrival:</em> If this is set, the consist broadcasts its condition number to the selected line or consist when arriving at this stop. <em>Clear stored triggers on departure:</em> If this is set, all stored triggers are discarded when the consist departs; otherwise only the triggers waited for here are discarded. <em>Trigger one consist only:</em> If this is set, only the earliest-arriving waiting consist on the target line is released.
</p>
<p>
The next three controls work together. They are described in full in <a href="consist_recombination.txt">Consist Recombination</a>.
</p>
<p>
<em>Couple at this stop:</em> This consist takes vehicles from the selected <a href="linemanagement.txt">line</a> or <a href="convoiinfo.txt">consist</a> at this stop. The <a href="consist_order.txt">consist order</a> for this entry describes the train after the transfer. The transfer happens when the other consist is at this stop and loading; if it exists but has not arrived, this consist waits for it without a time limit. See <a href="couple_divide.txt">Couple and Divide at This Stop</a>.
</p>
<p>
<em>Divide at this stop:</em> This consist gives vehicles to the selected line or consist at this stop. The consist order for this entry describes the part that stays on this schedule; the selected entry's order describes the part that joins the selected line or consist. The split-off part departs only if its order can be completed into a powered consist (a consist with at least one powered vehicle); otherwise the displaced vehicles are left at the stop as <a href="loose_vehicles.txt">loose vehicles</a>. If no target is selected, the displaced vehicles are also left at the stop as loose vehicles. See <a href="couple_divide.txt">Couple and Divide at This Stop</a>.
</p>
<p>
<em>Modify consist at this stop</em>: This defines the consist that should exist after the selected schedule entry. On arrival, vehicles are added, removed or substituted during a shunting pause (the pause in which the game changes the vehicles in the consist) to match the specified order. Each slot accepts specific vehicles or rule-based alternatives (for example, any vehicle within power and capacity limits), edited with the Add rule buttons. Available in line and consist schedules. See <a href="consist_order.txt">Modify Consist at This Stop</a>.
</p>
<p>
<em>Mirror schedule:</em> If this option is selected, the consists using this schedule will reverse the direction in which they follow the schedule when they get to the end of it. So, for example, if a line has the schedule A>B>C, if this button is not selected, the consist will go A>B>C>A>B>C, etc. If this is selected, the consist will instead travel A>B>C>B>A>B, etc.<br>
<em>Tip:</em> If this option is used with trains on double-track railways, the game will automatically work out which direction platform should be used, but this does mean that the exact platform cannot always be specified when this option is enabled.
</p>
<p>
<em>Alternate directions:</em> If this option is selected, each consist assigned to this line will start in the opposite end of the schedule to the last consist assigned to this line. This is useful for making sure that consists are evenly spaced on a line.
</p>
<p>
<em>Add</em> <a href="station.txt"><em>Stop:</em></a> This places a stop used by vehicles to pick up or drop off goods and passengers (water transport vehicles may use any square of water in the catchment area of a dock) or waypoint on the route.
To add a stop or waypoint: click on the option to select (which changes the cursor to a "stop" arrow), then click the required point in the main game window for the stop or waypoint. It must be a point on road, track or water as appropriate for type of vehicle. A stop placed outside a station will automatically create a waypoint.<br>
<em>Tip:</em> Vehicles do not pick up or drop off goods and passengers at waypoints.
</p>
<p>
<em>Insert:</em> This inserts a stop or waypoint into a schedule immediately before the currently selected stop or waypoint. To insert a stop or waypoint, click on an existing stop or waypoint in list to select it, then click on the insert button (which changes the cursor to a "stop" arrow), then click the required point in the main game window.
</p>
<p>
<em>Remove:</em> option deletes a stop or waypoint from the schedule. Click on the "remove", then click on a stop or waypoint in the schedule to remove it.
</p>
<p>
<em>Promote to</em> <a href="linemanagement.txt"><em>Line:</em></a> (only available when the <strong>Schedule</strong> window is opened from the <a href="convoiinfo.txt">Consist Information</a> window) creates a new line using the current schedule.
</p>
