<title>Consist Information Help</title>

<h1><strong>Consist Information</strong></h1>

<p>
<strong>Consist Information</strong> provides information about consists in the game. <em>Consists</em> are the individual units of transport that move around the map following schedules and transporting goods and passengers. A consist can be a single vehicle (such as a 'bus, an aircraft or a ship) or several coupled vehicles (such as a railway train consisting of a locomotive and carriages, a lorry with a trailer, a tug with barges or a horse and carriage). In order to move around the map, consists need at least one powered vehicle, but may also have one or more unpowered vehicles.
</p>
<p>
Older versions of Simutrans called a consist a convoy or convoi. The terms refer to the same object.
</p>
<p>
The consist information window lists the items (goods, passengers or mail) carried by a consist and also has controls to access further details, to and sell upgrade and the consist; to follow the Consist in the main game window; to change the name and <a href="schedule.txt">Schedule</a> of the consist; and to <a href="replace.txt">replace</a> it and like consists with different vehicles.
</p>
<p>
Consists are assembled and purchased from <a href="depot.txt">depots</a>, or by <a href="replace.txt">replacing</a> existing consists.
</p>
<p>
Click on a consist in the main game window with the <a href="inspection_tool.txt">inspection tool</a> or click on a consist listd in the <a href="convoi.txt">cehicle list</a> or the <a href="linemanagement.txt">line management</a> window to open the <strong>consist information</strong> window.<br>
The title bar of the <strong>consist information</strong> window shows a unique ID number (in brackets) and the name of the consist (by default, this is the name of the type of the first vehicle added to the consist, but this can be changed).
</p>
<p>
The <strong>consist information</strong> window contains a name box; a mini-view; information about the consist; option buttons; and a list of all mail, passengers or goods currently carried (if all items are not visible <a href="mouse.txt">re-size</a> the <strong>consist information</strong> window or use slider bars to scroll the items).
</p>
<p>
Information shown at the top of the <strong>consist information</strong> window includes:
</p>
<p>
<em>Internal ID</em>: a unique ID number for the consist, assigned by default when the consist is first purchased or assembled.
</p>
<p>
- <em>Name</em>: shows the name assigned to the consist. By default, the name is the name of the type of the first the vehicle added to the consist. To change the name, cick on the name box and enter a new name.
</p>
<p>
- <em>Speed</em>: the current speed in kilometres per hour. The consist's maximum speed is shown in brackets. A consist's maximum speed is the maximum speed of the slowest vehicle in the consist. Note that the maximum speed of the consist might be greater than the maximum speed of the way, in which case, the consist will not exceed the way's limit. A green progress bar indicates the current speed relative to the consist's maximum speed.
</p>
<p>
- <em>Revenue:</em> the profit made by the consist in the current game calendar year. In brackets after that is the variable operating cost of the whole consist per tile. Note that there might also be fixed costs per month that are not shown in this display.
</p>
<p>
- <em>Weight:</em> current combined weight of the vehicles in the consist and items carried, in metric tonnes. In brackets is the current weight of items (goods, passengers or mail) carried. A green progress bar indicates the proportion of the consist's carrying capacity currently in use. If the bar is yellow instead of green, this indicates that the consist is <a href="schedule.txt">waiting</a> for a set minimum load. <br>
<em>Tip:</em> To set or change wait levels for all consists in a line, use the update line option button in the <a href="linemanagement.txt">line management</a> window. To set or change the wait level for individual consists, use the <em>Schedule</em> button in the <strong>Consist Information</strong> or <strong>depot</strong> windows.
</p>
<p>
- <em>Destination:</em> the next <a href="station.txt">stop</a> or waypoint in the consist's <a href="schedule.txt">schedule</a>. A green progress bar indicates the progress of the consist towards the next destination.
</p>
<p>
The status line can show <em>Waiting for a coupling partner</em> when the consist is waiting at a stop for another consist, and <em>Shunting</em> during the pause in which the game rearranges the vehicles in the consist. Both belong to <a href="consist_recombination.txt">consist recombination</a>.
</p>
<p>
- <em>Serves line:</em> indicates any <a href="linemanagement.txt">line</a> assigned to the consist. Click the grey arrow next to the name of the line to open the line in the <a href="linemanagement.txt">line management</a> window.
</p>
<p>
- <em>Mini-view</em> shows the consist moving around in the game world. Click on the mini-view to centre the main game window on the consist.
</p>
<p>
Click on the following buttons in to open controls or select options:
</p>
<p>
<a href="schedule.txt"><em>Schedule:</em></a> opens a window with controls to change the route and other scheduling options for the consist. <em>Note</em>: changing a schedule on a consist assigned to a line will change the behaviour of only that consist, not other consists in the line, and will also de-assign the consist from the line. To change the schedule of the whole line, use the <a href="linemanagement.txt">line management</a> window instead.
</p>
<p>
<em>Go to depot:</em> sends the consist to the nearest appropriate <a href="depot.txt">depot</a>. Any items being conveyed are lost. In the depot, the consist keeps its <a href="schedule.txt">schedule</a> and any assigned <a href="linemanagement.txt">line</a>.
</p>
<p>
<em>Follow me:</em> when selected, this causes the main game window to track the movements of the consist. To turn off consist following, click on the button again or left click anywhere in the on <a href="map.txt">main game window</a>.
</p>
<p>
<em>Replace:</em> opens the <a href="replace.txt">replace</a> window, which allows the consist, either individually, or along with all other like consists in the line, or all other like consists owned by the same player, to be replaced automatically.
</p>
<p>
<em>Reverse route:</em> determines the order in which this consist follows its schedule. If this is not selected, the consist will follow its schedule from the first item in the list to the last item in the list. If this button is selected, will follow its schedule from the last item in the list to the first item in the list. For information on how to set up consists and lines to alternate between forward and reverse running automatically, see the information about the <a href="schedule.txt">schedule</a> window.
</p>
<p>
<em>Chart:</em> click on the "chart" button to toggle graphs showing statistics relating to the consist over the last twelve months. Click one or more of the coloured buttons to select which statistics are displayed (colours on the graph correspond to colours of the buttons pressed):<br>
- <em>Free Capacity</em> the proportion of the consist that is running empty. This might be negative if the consist is regularly overcrowded;<br>
- <em>Transported</em> the quantity of goods, passengers or mail transported;<br>
- <em>Average speed</em> the average speed of the consist;<br>
- <em>Comfort</em> the average comfort of vehicles in the consist, adjusted for overcrowding and any catering present in the consist;<br>
- <em>Revenue</em> the gross income received from this consist's transport activities;<br>
- <em>Running costs</em> the costs of operating this consist, being a total of (1) the distance dependant costs; and (2) the monthly fixed costs;<br>
- <em>Profit</em> the net profit earned by this consist (total revenue less total costs).<br>
- <em>Distance</em> the total distance (in kilometres) that this consist has travelled.
</p>
<p>
<em>Details</em> shows additional <a href="convoidetail.txt">details</a> about the consist and its vehicles, including some additional options.
</p>
<p>
<em>Sort Passengers/freight by:</em> lists the items currently carried by the consist.<br>
Information shown includes the quantity carried and capacity of the consist, the type of item carried, the final destination and next interchange <a href="station.txt">stop</a>. Note that, if the vehicle is overcrowded, the number currently being conveyed might be higher than the capacity.<br>
Use the button (which changes its text depending on what option is selected) to sort items in order of:<br>
 - <em>destination:</em> sorted alphabetically by the ultimate destination of the goods, passengers or mail carried;<br>
 - <em>via (detail):</em> sorted alphabetically by the next interchange of the goods, passengers or mail carried, also showing information about final destination;<br>
 - <em>via (amount):</em> sorted alphabetically by the next interchange of the goods, passengers or mail carried, with no other information;<br>
 - <em>amount:</em> sorted in numerical (descending) order of the number of each item carried by destination;<br>
 - <em>origin (detail):</em> sorted alphabetically by the ultimate destination of the goods, passengers or mail carried, also showing infromation on the origins of those items; and<br>
 - <em>origin (amount):</em> sorted in numerical (descending) order of the number of each item carried by origin.
</p>
